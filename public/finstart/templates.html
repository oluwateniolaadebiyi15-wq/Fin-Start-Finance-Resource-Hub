Notes & Reminders

← Review Budgeting Rules

Press Ctrl+P or Cmd+P to print this worksheet

📋 How to use

Print the worksheet above. Use a pen to fill in your income and planned expenses before the month begins. Review actuals at month end.

📌 Pro tip

Fill in your savings row first (Pay Yourself First rule), then divide what remains between your needs and wants categories.

🎯 Targets

Using 50/30/20? Your Needs total should be ≤ 50% of income, Wants ≤ 30%, and Savings ≥ 20%.
