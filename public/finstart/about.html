🌱 Our Story

Finance Should Be Simple.
We Made It That Way.

FinStart began the way most meaningful things do — with frustration. Three young Nigerians sat in a room and realised they had each been handed the same invisible disadvantage: no one had ever taught them what to actually do with money. Not really.

They had degrees. They had ambition. But the gap between earning money and building something lasting with it was wide, dark, and poorly lit. Most finance resources assumed you already understood the language. Most advice assumed you had money to start with.

So they built the resource they wished had existed when they were 15. No paywalls, no jargon, no condescension — just the clearest, most honest guide to personal finance for anyone starting from wherever they happen to be.

What We Stand For

Mission, Vision & Motto

Three statements that keep us honest about why FinStart exists and where we're going.

🎯 Mission

To democratise personal finance education for every young adult — making budgeting knowledge as accessible as a conversation with a trusted friend, regardless of background, income, or starting point.

🔭 Vision

A generation of financially confident young adults who make informed money decisions — not because they were lucky enough to have wealthy parents, but because they had access to the right knowledge at the right time.

💬 Motto

"Start Small. Think Big. Live Free."

Every wealthy journey began with a single, deliberate decision. Yours starts here.

🔥 Our Goal & Drive

We're Driven by the Gap Between Ambition and Financial Know-How

Too many brilliant, hardworking young people are held back not by a lack of ability — but by a lack of financial vocabulary. We watched people earn more money only to fall deeper into confusion. We saw first-hand how the absence of basic budgeting knowledge turns early adult life into a cycle of survival instead of a platform for building.

FinStart's goal is simple: close that gap. Not with a course that costs ₦50,000. Not with a workshop you have to commute to. With a free, always-on resource that meets you exactly where you are — whether you're earning ₦30,000 or $3,000 a month.

📚

Educate

Provide the clearest, most beginner-friendly finance content available — anywhere, for free.

⚡

Empower

Give every reader the tools, frameworks, and confidence to take immediate action on their finances.

🌍

Expand Access

Reach young adults across Africa and beyond who have been underserved by traditional financial education.

👥 The Founding Team

Meet the People Behind FinStart

Three founders. One shared belief: that the right financial knowledge, delivered simply, can reshape a generation's relationship with money.

Adebiyi Oluwateniola

CEO & Founder

Teniola didn't set out to build a fintech platform. He set out to answer one question that had haunted him since his first paycheck: why does money always run out before the month does? The answer — buried in budgeting books no one in his circle had ever read — became the seed of FinStart.

A natural systems thinker with a restless urgency to share what he learns, Teniola leads FinStart with the same principle he lives by: that clarity is the most underrated form of generosity.

"I built the resource I needed at 19 — so the next 19-year-old doesn't have to figure it out alone."

Abdulgafur Ibrahim

Co-Founder · Strategy & Growth

Gafur has always believed that the best strategies are the ones people actually follow — which is why he obsesses less over perfection and more over simplicity. He joined FinStart because he saw something rare: a mission that didn't need to be oversold, only properly built.

His fingerprints are on every decision that keeps FinStart focused. He's the voice in the room that asks, "Would a 22-year-old with ₦15,000 in savings actually use this?" — and doesn't move on until the answer is yes.

"Financial freedom isn't a destination. It's a habit you build one intentional decision at a time."

Omoniyi Abdulakeem

Co-Founder · Content & Community

Akeem is proof that the right words can change a financial trajectory. He grew up watching people around him make money decisions driven not by strategy, but by fear — and decided early that education, not income, was the real lever.

As FinStart's content lead, he shapes how complex ideas are translated into the kind of language that sticks. He writes for the person who has never finished a finance book — and makes sure they don't need to, because everything they need is already here.

"When people understand money, they stop being afraid of it. That's when everything changes."
